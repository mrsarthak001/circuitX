FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee to attend?
No. CircuitX is free to attend — your Jetson kit, CraftifAI platform access, meals and refreshments are all covered. Bring your laptop and build.
Who is CircuitX for?
Builders who already ship. You'll work on NVIDIA Jetson kits and CraftifAI's tooling (AgentIQ, PipeGen, FirmGen, Orbit Suite) over clean APIs — no soldering — with mentors on hand to unblock you rather than cover fundamentals. If you're comfortable with code and moving fast on real hardware, you'll be right at home.
Is this solo or team-based?
CircuitX is a solo event — you apply and build individually. There are no teams to form or coordinate. Every builder gets their own Jetson kit and full platform access, and everything you ship is yours.
What kinds of projects are a good fit?
On-device vision pipelines, autonomous control loops, smart-sensing rigs, real-time edge AI that senses, decides and acts. Ambitious and unconventional ideas are welcome — the one requirement is that it runs on the hardware by submission lock.
What should I bring?
Just your laptop and chargers. Your Jetson kit, sensors, platform credits and meals are all provided on-site.
How are projects judged?
A CraftifAI panel judges on working prototypes — does it run, does it use the hardware meaningfully, and would someone actually want it. Shortlisted builders demo live on stage before awards.
Where is the venue?
Microsoft Campus. The exact address, entry gate and logistics are shared with accepted builders ahead of the event.
