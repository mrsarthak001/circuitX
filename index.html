FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee to attend?
No. Embedded is free to attend. Your hardware kit, FirmGen & PipeGen access, meals and refreshments are all covered. Bring your laptop and build.
Who is Embedded for?
Builders who already ship. You'll work on real hardware (NVIDIA Jetson for PipeGen, or ESP32 & STM32 boards for FirmGen) with CraftifAI's Orbit products over clean SDKs, with mentors on hand to unblock you rather than cover fundamentals. If you're comfortable with code and moving fast on real hardware, you'll be right at home.
Is this solo or team-based?
Embedded is a solo event; you apply and build individually. There are no teams to form or coordinate. Every builder gets their own hardware kit and full SDK access, and everything you ship is yours.
What kinds of projects are a good fit?
Embedded firmware for ESP32 & STM32, on-device vision pipelines, smart-sensing rigs, real-time edge AI that senses, decides and acts. Ambitious and unconventional ideas are welcome; the one requirement is that it runs on the hardware by submission lock.
What should I bring?
Just your laptop and chargers. Your hardware kit, sensors, SDK credits and meals are all provided on-site.
How are projects judged?
A CraftifAI panel judges on working prototypes: does it run, does it use the hardware meaningfully, and would someone actually want it. Shortlisted builders demo live on stage before awards.
Where is the venue?
Microsoft Office, Luxor North Tower, Bengaluru. The exact floor, entry gate and logistics are shared with accepted builders ahead of the event.
