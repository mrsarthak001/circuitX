PipeGen

Describe a vision system in plain English. Walk out with it running on real hardware.
What it is

PipeGen is CraftifAI's agentic platform that turns a natural language description into a production-ready vision pipeline for NVIDIA edge hardware.

You describe the problem. PipeGen proposes, generates, and runs the pipeline. That leaves you the whole sprint to build something real on top of it.

The challenge

Build the most inventive working application powered by a live perception pipeline.

Requirement: participants must bring their own Ubuntu laptop with an NVIDIA GPU.

Need a spark?

Counting and tracking, depth sensing, lane detection, segmentation. Or something we have not thought of. Originality wins.

What you need
Jetson AGX OrinJetson Orin Nanoor x86 desktop + NVIDIA GPU~50 GB free diskLinux environment
How it's judged
  • Working demo. Runs end to end on hardware, live input to real output. A finished simple system beats an ambitious broken one.
  • Innovation. A use case we have not seen before.
  • Beyond the pipeline. What you built on top: application logic, alerts, dashboards, integrations, tuning.
  • Real-world impact. Who needs this, and how clearly your demo makes that case.
